# Third-party boundary

## Tesseract by Mirage

Tesseract is **not bundled** with PISTE Studio.

PISTE Studio only invokes a separately installed `tsrct` executable through its documented command-line interface and consumes the schemas/output returned by that executable.

No Tesseract source code, binary, plugin, skill package, font, FFmpeg bundle, or other Mirage-distributed component is included in this repository/package.

Users are responsible for obtaining Tesseract from Mirage's official distribution channel and complying with the applicable Mirage terms and third-party notices.
